## Time

- **The day before high-school graduation** (卒業の前日), per the official award synopsis.
- After this, Ibuki is going **abroad for university**, which is why this evening feels like the last chance.

## Place

- **A Japanese high school** and its **student council**. The council has just two members, Ibuki and Yuri.
- **The road home**, walked together after school. Most of the interruptions happen here.
- The town is never named in the sources we checked. Any specific real-world location would be <Unverified lang="en" />.

## Weather and atmosphere

The official synopsis stresses a **downpour** (土砂降りの雨). This site's visual identity borrows from that: diagonal rain lines, a night palette for dark mode, and a single red accent for the confession.

## Genre layer

The setting is grounded realism (school, rain, a mugger) until a **science-fiction intrusion**: an alien threat to the entire planet. Reviewers describe this sudden genre escalation as the core of the comedy.
