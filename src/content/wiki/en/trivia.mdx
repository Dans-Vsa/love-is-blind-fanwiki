- **Debut work.** *Love is Blind* is Fujimoto's first published manga. Jump SQ.'s own editors called it his debut when they re-released it in 2016.
- **The judge was Takeshi Konomi**, creator of *The Prince of Tennis*. He judged the 9th Crown Newcomer Manga Award, where the story took an Honorable Mention (佳作) and ¥300,000.
- **"Half the pages."** Konomi's comment praised the story's momentum but said it could be told in half the pages. Fujimoto's own afterword in *17-21* recalls the editors saying he took 31 pages for what could be done in 16.
- **Age 21, from Yamagata.** The award record lists Fujimoto as 21 and submitting from Yamagata, where he was studying at Tohoku University of Art and Design.
- **Not his first submission.** *A Couple Clucking Chickens…* (2011) and *Sasaki Stopped a Bullet* (2013) came earlier, but *Love is Blind* was the first to be printed.
- **Second life on Jump+.** On 29 August 2016 it was re-released for a limited time on Shōnen Jump+ as a "special one-shot" during *Fire Punch*'s run.
- **"Osu!"** Jump SQ.'s editors highlighted the protagonist driven by overflowing emotion, and the vice-president who answers "Osu!" (押忍) and follows him.
- **Best-written in the book?** The Comics Journal judged it probably the best-written piece in *17-21*.
- **Same studio, back to back.** In the anime, episodes 2 (*Sasaki Stopped a Bullet*) and 3 (*Love is Blind*) were both made by Lapin Track with the same scriptwriter, Teruko Utsumi.
- **Not the reality show.** The title has nothing to do with the Netflix dating series *Love Is Blind* (including its Japanese edition). Search engines mix them up constantly.
- **Original English title wording.** VIZ prints it as "Love is Blind". Some fan databases use "Love Is Blind" or the romanisation *Koi wa Moumoku*.
