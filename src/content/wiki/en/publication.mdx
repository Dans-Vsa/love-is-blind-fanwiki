## The *17-21* collection

The one-shot is collected as the **third of four stories** in the first of Fujimoto's two short-story volumes.

| # | Story | Year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A Couple Clucking Chickens Were Still Kickin' in the Schoolyard | 2011 |
| 2 | Sasaki Stopped a Bullet | 2013 |
| 3 | **Love is Blind** | 2013 (award) / 2014 (print) |
| 4 | Shikaku | 2014 |

- **Japan:** 藤本タツキ短編集 17-21, Shueisha (Jump Comics+), 4 October 2021, ISBN 978-4-08-882803-9.
- **English:** *Tatsuki Fujimoto Before Chainsaw Man: 17-21*, VIZ Media (Shonen Jump), 17 January 2023, ISBN 978-1-9747-3477-1, 168 pages.
- **Companion volume:** *22-26* (Japan 4 Nov 2021; VIZ 18 Apr 2023): Mermaid Rhapsody, Woke-Up-as-a-Girl Syndrome, Nayuta of the Prophecy, Sisters.

The titles *17-21* and *22-26* refer to Fujimoto's age when he drew the stories, as The Comics Journal and Amazon's anime announcement both describe. That matches the award record, which lists him as 21 for *Love is Blind*.

### Reception of the episode

Reviews were mostly warm. The People's Movies compared its visual style (fast title cards, shifting aspect ratios, flat colour blocks) to *Monogatari*. ComicBook.com was the outlier and ranked it last of the eight episodes, calling it mainly comic relief.
