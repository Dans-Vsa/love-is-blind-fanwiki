## Premise (spoiler-free)

*Love is Blind* (恋は盲目, *Koi wa Mōmoku*) is a 31-page comedic one-shot. It is Tatsuki Fujimoto's first published manga.

It is **the day before high-school graduation**. Student council president **Ibuki** has made up his mind: today he will confess to his underclassman **Yuri Kōnosu**, the vice-president and the council's only other member. Ibuki is about to leave Japan to study at a university abroad, so the walk home with her may be the last time he sees her.

The official award synopsis sums up what happens next: in a **pouring rain**, a **robber** and even **aliens** barge in. Ibuki still tries to push his feelings through. Every crisis gets the same answer from him: something else matters more today.

<SpeechBubble cite="Ibuki's refrain, as quoted in English-language reviews of the VIZ edition">
  “…that's the least of my priorities!”
</SpeechBubble>

## Genre and tone

The story is a **gag romantic comedy**. Each obstacle is bigger than the last, ending at literal planetary stakes, while the emotional core stays one small, sincere sentence. Japanese readers have described it as a "new-generation *sekai-kei* gag comedy". *Sekai-kei* means "world-type": stories where a personal relationship sits next to world-ending stakes.

## Full plot summary

<Spoiler lang="en" title="Full plot, including the ending">

1. **The resolve.** On the eve of graduation, Ibuki decides that confessing to Yuri outranks all of his duties as president. He asks her to walk home with him.
2. **First interruption: the graduation ceremony.** Someone needs Ibuki's help with the graduation ceremony. Reviews disagree on whether this is a student or a teacher <Unverified lang="en" />. The anime cast includes a teacher, Hayasaka-sensei. Ibuki brushes it off.
3. **Second interruption: the robber.** In the downpour, a mugger holds them up. Reviews say the mugger wants Ibuki's clothes, and Ibuki's school uniform ends up changing hands <Unverified lang="en" note="Detail reported by TV Tropes and one review; not confirmed against the book." />. Ibuki refuses to let even a robbery derail him.
4. **Third interruption: the aliens.** An alien appears and announces that Earth is about to be invaded and destroyed. Ibuki declares that the end of the planet is the least of his priorities. In the heat of the moment he says out loud that he is about to confess, and when Yuri asks to whom, he answers: to you.
5. **The ending.** The sincerity of the confession turns the whole situation around. According to TV Tropes, the robber lets them go (even settling up over the uniform), and the alien calls off, or at least postpones, the destruction of Earth. One English-language review likens the closing cutaway to Akira Toriyama's short stories.

</Spoiler>

## How it reads

The structure is a classic **escalation gag**. The same beat repeats three times with ever larger stakes, then a payoff releases all the tension at once. The Anitay review praises the confession as superbly executed after the previous close calls. Fujimoto himself later joked in the *17-21* afterword that editors told him he took 31 pages for something that could be done in 16. The award judge said much the same (see [Trivia](../trivia/)).
