## Story timeline

<Spoiler lang="en" title="Story beats, from resolve to ending">

| # | Beat | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Resolve | The day before graduation, Ibuki decides to confess to Yuri today, whatever happens. |
| 2 | Invitation | He asks Yuri to walk home with him; it may be their last time together before he leaves to study abroad. |
| 3 | Interruption #1 | A matter concerning the graduation ceremony (student or teacher: sources differ <Unverified lang="en" />). Dismissed. |
| 4 | Interruption #2 | A robber, in the pouring rain. Dismissed: there are bigger things today. |
| 5 | Interruption #3 | An alien announces the invasion and destruction of Earth. Dismissed: the least of his priorities. |
| 6 | Slip | In the heat of the moment Ibuki blurts out that he is about to confess. Yuri asks to whom. "To you." |
| 7 | Resolution | The confession disarms everyone: the robber relents and the alien calls off (or postpones) the invasion. |

</Spoiler>

## Real-world timeline

| Date | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Nov 2013 | *Love is Blind* wins an Honorable Mention (佳作, "Monthly TOP") at the **9th Crown Newcomer Manga Award**. Fujimoto is 21 and submits from Yamagata. 31 pages; judge Takeshi Konomi. |
| 2014 | Published in **Jump SQ.19 Vol. 13** (Shueisha) as Fujimoto's **debut**. English Wikipedia gives 19 April 2014. |
| 29 Aug 2016 | Re-released for a limited time on **Shōnen Jump+** as a "special one-shot", alongside *Fire Punch*. |
| 4 Oct 2021 | Collected as the 3rd story of **藤本タツキ短編集 17-21** (Shueisha, ISBN 978-4-08-882803-9). |
| Jun 2022 | VIZ Media licenses both short-story volumes. |
| 17 Jan 2023 | English release: ***Tatsuki Fujimoto Before Chainsaw Man: 17-21*** (VIZ, ISBN 978-1-9747-3477-1). |
| Sep 2025 | Anime anthology ***Tatsuki Fujimoto 17-26*** announced. |
| 5 Oct 2025 | World premiere at the Global Stage Hollywood Film Festival. |
| 17 Oct 2025 | Two-week limited theatrical run in Japan. |
| 7–8 Nov 2025 | Worldwide streaming on Amazon Prime Video. Episode 3 is *Love is Blind*. Listed dates differ by time zone. |
