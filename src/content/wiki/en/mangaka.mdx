## Awards (selection)

- **66th Shogakukan Manga Award** (Best Shōnen) for *Chainsaw Man*.
- **Harvey Award, Best Manga** for *Chainsaw Man*: 2021, 2022, 2023.
- ***Kono Manga ga Sugoi!*** #1: *Chainsaw Man* (2021 list) and *Look Back* (2022 list, male readers).

## Influences

Fujimoto often names **films** as influences: *The Chaser*, *The Raid*, *Sadako vs. Kayako*, Takeshi Kitano's films and *The Texas Chain Saw Massacre*. His manga influences include Hiroaki Samura, Hideki Arai and Tsutomu Nihei. **Fan analysis:** That cinematic sensibility is already visible in *Love is Blind*'s pacing. Wide establishing beats are followed by sudden "cuts" to an absurd new threat.
